# Traveller (Mongoose 2nd Edition)

A traveller sheet for **Mongoose Traveller 2nd Edition**, with a custom HTML
layout. Its tabs and its arithmetic follow J. Brannen's Traveller character
spreadsheet.

The JSON and HTML are generated by
[`scripts/traveller/build_sheet.py`](../../scripts/traveller/build_sheet.py) -
edit that and run it, then `scripts/build_index.py`. The stylesheet is written by
hand.

## What it covers

Seven pages, one for each of the spreadsheet's tabs:

- **Profile** - name, species, height, weight, age and appearance; the homeworld
  with its UWP, bases, trade codes, travel zone and gas giant; and the
  **lifepath**, one row per term with its career, assignment, survival,
  commission, advancement, rank and events
- **Characteristics & Skills** - the six characteristics as base, misc and
  injury with their **dice modifier**, and the **UPP** written out from them in
  hex (with PSI once you have it). **Psionics** with each talent's check.
  Radiation and its permanent END loss, disease, fatigue, and a study period.
  **Every skill and speciality** with its level, misc bonus, total and its check
  DM against each of the six characteristics. Language, Profession and four
  custom lines take names of your own. Total skill levels are counted against
  `3 x (EDU + INT)`
- **Combat & Equipment** - eight weapons, each with the **attack DM** its skill
  gives; armour with its total protection; augments; initiative, Tactics,
  Leadership and the dodge penalty you impose; **equipment** with quantity, mass
  and cost; **encumbrance** against STR + END + Athletics (Strength) and
  (Endurance); cash, pension, salary, mortgages, debts and savings; and
  **standard of living** - what your SOC suits, what you chose, and its monthly
  cost
- **Vehicles** - two, each with its skill check DM (skill + DEX DM + agility),
  speeds and cruising speed and range, armour and hull, systems, traits, weapons
  and stored cargo against its capacity
- **Spacecraft** - the ship's components, thrust and jump, hull points, crew, and
  **power allocation** with what basic systems and each drive require for its
  tonnage; weapons; stored cargo against its hold
- **Background & Personality** - descriptor opposites, goals, emotions,
  favourites, appearance, family and other relationships, childhood, training and
  social class
- **Campaign Notes** - the party, allies, contacts, rivals and enemies, other
  NPCs, organisations, locations and notes

### Skill arithmetic

A skill you have not trained uses the **unskilled** penalty: -3, reduced by your
Jack-of-All-Trades level. Training a skill at 0, or any one of its specialities,
makes every speciality of that skill usable at 0. A skill's check DM against a
characteristic is its total plus that characteristic's dice modifier; attacks
use DEX for guns and heavy weapons and the better of STR and DEX in melee.

The spreadsheet's **Spacecraft (Extensive)** tab is a ship *design* calculator.
That is a construction procedure rather than a character's record, so it is not
here; the Spacecraft page is the spreadsheet's Barebones one.

**Every derived value can be set by hand**, and every warning - more skill
levels than your EDU and INT allow, more cargo than a hold takes, carrying more
than twice your threshold - is only a warning.

## No content pack

There is no open licence for the Traveller rules, so there is no catalogue of
careers, weapons or equipment. Everything is a plain field or list.
