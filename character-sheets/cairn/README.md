# Cairn

A character sheet for **Cairn**, Yochai Gal's rules-light dark-fantasy
adventure game.

Cairn characters have three ability scores, no classes and no levels, so the
whole sheet fits comfortably in the default sectioned layout — no custom HTML
needed.

## What it covers

- **Abilities** — Strength, Dexterity, Willpower
- **Hit Protection** — HP and Max HP (1d6 at creation, so both cap at 6),
  Armour, Deprived, plus a reminder of what happens at 0 HP and a
  damage-reduction note that caps at 3
- **Possessions** — gold and a ten-slot inventory
- **Notes**

HP is **Hit Protection**, not health: it is a PC's ability to avoid damage, and
damage past it carries into STR. The sheet reflects that rather than implying a
pool of health, because the distinction is the mechanic.
