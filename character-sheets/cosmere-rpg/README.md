# Cosmere RPG

A character sheet for Brandon Sanderson's **Cosmere Roleplaying Game**, with a
custom HTML layout following the content and order of the Mistborn campaign
setting character sheet, in original styling.

## What it covers

Two pages, as printed:

- **Character** - name, level, paths and ancestry; the **six attributes** in their
  three realms, each pair with its **defense** (10 + the two attributes);
  **health**, **focus** and **Investiture** with their maximums; **deflect**; the
  **eighteen skills**, each with its ranks as five boxes to tick and its modifier
  (attribute + ranks), plus three lines for skills such as Allomancy, with an
  attribute you choose; **lifting capacity** (and carrying capacity),
  **movement**, **recovery die** and **senses range**; conditions and injuries,
  weapons, expertises and talents
- **Story & Gear** - appearance, armor and equipment, **boxings**, purpose,
  obstacle, nine **goals** with three progress boxes each, notes and connections

### The arithmetic

- **Health** starts at 10 + Strength, then rises 5 a level to level 5, 4 to 10, 3
  to 15, 2 to 20 and 1 at 21, adding Strength again at levels 6, 11 and 16
- **Focus** is 2 + Willpower; **Investiture**, once you tick *Invested*, is 2 + the
  higher of Awareness and Presence
- Lifting capacity, movement, recovery die and senses range step up every two
  points of Strength, Speed, Willpower and Awareness respectively
- **Warnings** - never blocks - when attribute points or skill ranks exceed what
  the level gives, or a skill has more ranks than the tier allows (2, 3, 4, then 5)

Every derived value can be set by hand, and talents and bonuses that change them
are yours to apply.

## No content pack

The Cosmere RPG's rules are not openly licensed (Brotherwise plans a generic
Plotweaver SRD, without Dragonsteel's settings), so there is no catalogue of
paths, talents or equipment.
